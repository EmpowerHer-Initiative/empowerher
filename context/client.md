# Client Profile

**Name:** EmpowerHer
**Industry:** Nonprofit — Education & Women's Empowerment
**Products/Services:** Mentorship programs, creative writing workshops, storytelling platform (HerVoice), leadership development, cultural exchange programs, educational support for Afghan girls
**Target Audience:** Afghan girls and women (primarily aged 14+) denied access to education, plus volunteers and partner organizations worldwide
**Value Proposition:** Youth-led initiative empowering Afghan girls through mentorship, storytelling, and creative expression — giving them tools, confidence, and platforms to raise their voices
**Tone:** Warm, hopeful, empowering, resilient — formal but heartfelt, mission-driven
**Brand Colors:** #43a9e2 (primary blue), #c5bc42 (secondary gold)
**Key Differentiators:** Founded and led by Afghan youth (Mahdi Rahimi & Nahid Karimi) who lived through the fall of Kabul; fully virtual programs reaching girls inside Afghanistan; dual-program model (Mentorship + HerVoice publication platform); partnerships with NSHSS, AGFAF, Amplify Afghan Women
**Website:** https://www.empowerher-initiative.org
